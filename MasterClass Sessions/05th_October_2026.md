## Context: Why Cybersecurity?

- **October is Cybersecurity Awareness Month** — organised by **CERT-In** (Indian Computer Emergency Response Team) and the **Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY)**
- Theme for this year: **"Don't Make It Easy for Them"**
- Post-COVID, the shift to online services (banking, apps, social media) has significantly increased the attack surface for cybercriminals
- AI-generated scams and deepfakes are on the rise — cybersecurity is no longer just an IT professional's concern

## IBM SkillsBuild Registration — Step-by-Step

> *Use only the official link or QR code shared in the session/WhatsApp group.*

```
Step 1: Open the IBM SkillsBuild link (via QR code or chat link)
Step 2: Click "Sign Up with Google" (recommended)
         — If no Gmail: enter email ID, first name, last name manually
Step 3: Select your Google account from the pop-up → click Continue
Step 4: Select Country → India → click Continue
Step 5: Fill in your profile details
         ⚠️ Enter your name carefully — it will appear on your certificates
         ⚠️ If you have no last name, repeat your first name
Step 6: Confirm Sponsoring Organisation is "ACA BharatCares"
         (appears automatically — if not, reopen the link)
Step 7: Fill Additional Information (optional — can be skipped)
Step 8: Accept Terms & Conditions → click Submit
Step 9: "Success — Your account is created" pop-up appears
Step 10: Click "Go to SkillsBuild"
Step 11: Enroll in "Cybersecurity Bootcamp" → click Enroll
Step 12: Begin with Module 1 — Cybersecurity Fundamentals → Go to Activity
```

### Login (Returning Users)

- Do **not** click Sign Up again
- Click **"I already have an IBM account"** → log in
- Progress is **not lost** even if the tab closes
